WebScrcpy
Wireless Setup Guide

Mirror your Android phone in the browser — no cable, no IP addresses.

1Run the one-line Wi-Fi command in Terminal
2Open WebScrcpy and tap your phone under Nearby
3Allow the prompt on your phone
4Mirroring starts — unplug and stay wireless

webscrcpy.vercel.app · Works with Android 11+ · macOS & Windows

1Prepare your phone (one time)

Your phone needs USB debugging switched on. This takes 2 minutes and you never repeat it.

On your phoneDo this
1. Unlock developer modeSettings → About phone → tap Build number 7 times
2. Turn on debuggingSettings → Developer options → USB debugging ON
3. Wireless debuggingSettings → Developer options → Wireless debugging ON (Android 11+)
4. Same Wi-FiPhone and computer on the same Wi-Fi network
If you have no cable at all Skip to step 2 — Wireless debugging lets the phone announce itself on the network and you never need a cable.

2

2Run the one-line setup

This command installs a small helper on your computer so it can find your phone automatically.

Mac — open Terminal and paste:

curl -fsSL https://webscrcpy.vercel.app/connect-wifi.sh | bash

Windows — open PowerShell and paste:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://webscrcpy.vercel.app/connect-wifi.ps1 | iex"

The script looks for your phone on Wi-Fi first. If it finds it, you're done. If not, it asks you to plug in a cable for a one-time handoff.

3Keep the helper running (one time)

Install it as a background service so your phone appears under Nearby even after reboots.

Mac:

curl -fsSL https://webscrcpy.vercel.app/install-mac.sh | bash

Windows:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -c "irm https://webscrcpy.vercel.app/install-windows.ps1 | iex"
You only do this once After this, connecting is a single tap in the browser — no commands, no cable.

3

4Open WebScrcpy and click Connect

Go to webscrcpy.vercel.app and click Connect (top-right).

The start screen — click Connect in the top-right corner.

4

5Tap your phone under Nearby

A panel opens. Under Wi-Fi Devices you'll see Nearby — tap to connect with your phone's name. One tap is all it takes.

Your phone appears under Nearby — found automatically, no IP typing.

Don't see your phone? Unlock the phone and make sure Wireless debugging is ON, then close and reopen this panel — it rescans every time it opens.

5

6Allow the prompt on your phone

Your phone shows an Allow USB debugging? dialog. Unlock the phone and tap Allow (tick "Always allow" to skip this next time).

WebScrcpy waits while you approve the prompt on the phone screen.

6

7Mirroring starts

A few seconds after you tap Allow, your phone's screen appears live in the browser. You can now control it with mouse and keyboard.

You can unplug the cable now Once connected over Wi-Fi, the cable is no longer needed until the next reboot.

Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Nothing under NearbyWake the phone; toggle Wireless debugging off/on; reopen the panel
"did not authorize within 25s"Phone screen was locked — unlock it, then tap the phone again
"Helper not reachable"Run the install command from step 3 again
Wrong / old IP savedFixed automatically now — the entry heals when the phone is found
After a phone reboot Wireless mode resets. Run the connect command from step 2 once more — it will ask for the cable for a one-time handoff, then wireless works again.
